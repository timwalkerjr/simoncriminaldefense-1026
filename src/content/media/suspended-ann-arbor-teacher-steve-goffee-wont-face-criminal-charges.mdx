ANN ARBOR, MI - Tappan Middle School teacher Steve Goffee will not face any criminal charges after a six-month-long investigation by Ann Arbor police.

Ann Arbor Public Schools placed Goffee on paid administrative leave on April 25 from his position as physical education teacher at Tappan Middle School.
