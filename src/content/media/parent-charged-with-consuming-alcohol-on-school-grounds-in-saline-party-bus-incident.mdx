One Saline parent accused of drinking alcohol on a "party bus" on school property on prom night was charged with a misdemeanor Friday - although police reports indicate more than 20 parents were involved.

But police reports released under the Freedom of Information Act Friday show only that parent was positively identified by a police officer as having a beer in his hand that night......
