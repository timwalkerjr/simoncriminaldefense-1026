PITTSFIELD TOWNSHIP, MI - A son’s mental ability to contribute to his defense remains in question as he awaits trial in his father’s death.

A competency hearing for Michael B. Krause, 30, of Ann Arbor, was adjourned Thursday, April 11 when he appeared at Washtenaw County 14A-1 District Court.

He’s accused in the violent death of 66-year-old Ann Arbor dentist Michael E. Krause.
