WHITMORE LAKE, MI - A criminal case filed by the attorney general’s office last year against a Northfield Township police lieutenant and two relatives has been dropped in Livingston County.

Lt. Timothy Greene, 48, his brother John Greene, 44, of Wyandotte and his father, Robert Greene, 76, of Canton, had charges related to auto salvage vehicle titles dropped April 10 in Livingston County’s 53rd District Court - days before trial, according to attorneys in the case.
