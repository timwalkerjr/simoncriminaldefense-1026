**Defense Strategies in Domestic Violence Cases**

While there is no "one size fits all" strategy to defending domestic violence charges, key strategies include:

- **A thorough Client Intake Interview:** We begin by conducting a **detailed and confidential interview** to understand your side of the story and gather all information and evidence concerning the alleged incident.
- **Comprehensive Discovery:** We obtain and review **all available evidence**, including police reports, in-car and body-worn camera footage, 9-1-1 audio recordings.
- **Independent Investigation:** We doggedly search for any collateral sources of evidence such as text messages or security camera footage. A thorough investigation can uncover inconsistencies or provide crucial context to the events.
- **Pretrial Motions:** Where warranted, we argue pretrial motions to challenge evidence or due process rights.
- **Trial Preparation:** If your case proceeds to trial, we prepare you thoroughly for all courtroom proceedings, including potential testimony, ensuring your best possible defense at trial.
- **Presentation of Defenses:** There are several defenses to Domestic Violence charges. False allegations, consent, use of **reasonable force in self-defense,** mutual combat, to name a few. We carefully analyze the facts of your case to determine the best and most viable defense.

**Navigating the Complexities of a Domestic Violence Case**

It's important to understand that domestic violence cases often have unique legal and procedural aspects. Unlike other assault cases, prosecutors in Michigan often pursue these charges even if the alleged victim does not wish to press charges or proceed. It is the state, not the individual, who is the moving party. The moving party is the People of the State of Michigan (or local Township/City Attorney), and only the prosecutor has the authority to dismiss the case.
